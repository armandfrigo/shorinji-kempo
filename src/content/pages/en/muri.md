## Shorinji Kempo Muri — for children

The Muri dōjō specialises in **children's practice**. From age six, children learn in a safe, playful space to grow **strong — in body and mind**.

Founded in 2018, the group welcomes children of all levels, from newcomers to advanced students.

## 🎯 What children learn in Muri

**Physical:**
- basic techniques (safe strikes, kicks, throws)
- balance, flexibility, strength
- body awareness

**Mental:**
- **self-confidence** — “I can do this!”
- **focus** — concentration and discipline
- **respect** — for instructors, rules, and fellow students
- **perseverance** — meeting challenges steadily

**Social:**
- friendships with other children
- cooperation instead of competition
- clear, age-appropriate rules

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For parents

### Is Shorinji Kempo safe for my child?

**Yes.** The children's curriculum is designed with care:
- **no competition** — no tournaments or rough free-for-alls
- **partner practice** — always with a partner, not alone
- **age-suitable techniques** — no hard contact
- **constant supervision** — Tomoko watches attentively

Compared with many other activities, injuries are **very rare**.

### What age is best?

- **From six years**: a good age to begin
- **Six to twelve**: ideal for foundational growth
- **Teens (12+)**: may also train with adults in Aubonne or Basel

### What should my child bring?

- sport clothes (T-shirt and sweatpants)
- socks
- water

After a few weeks: a gi (~CHF 80–120)

### May I watch?

**Yes!** Parents may observe any time. Some parents join the practice; others simply watch.
