## Links & resources

Official organisations, partner federations, and useful material on Shorinji Kempo.

---

## 🌍 Official organisations

### World Shorinji Kempo Organization (WSKO)
The **world umbrella body** for Shorinji Kempo, founded in Japan in 1947.

**Website**: https://www.shorinjikempo.or.jp/wsko/

WSKO coordinates training, examinations, taikai (major gatherings), and exchange among the many countries where Shorinji Kempo is practised.

## 🥋 Practical resources

### International events (taikai)
Key annual gatherings worldwide:

- **European Taikai** — meeting of European federations
- **World Taikai** — largest international gathering (roughly every two to three years in Japan)
- **Swiss Taikai** — Swiss gathering (organised by our association)

Current dates appear on the [WSKO website](https://www.shorinjikempo.or.jp/wsko/).

### Black-belt training
Intensive sessions for black belts and advanced students.

Organised by our federation each year.

## 🎓 Further reading

### Official Shorinji Kempo documentation

The **Shorinji Kempo Kyōhan** (少林寺拳法教範) is the principal text documenting techniques, philosophy, and tradition.

Available in Japanese and other languages.

### Kempo Gokui (five core principles)

Five central teachings of Shorinji Kempo philosophy:

1. **Ken Zen Ichinyo** — body and mind are one
2. **Riki Ai Funi** — strength and love are inseparable
3. **Shushu Koju** — defence before attack
4. **Fusatsu Katsujin** — protect, do not destroy
5. **Go Ju Ittai** — hard and soft in balance
