## Power and balance: the two paths of self-defence

In Shorinji Kempo there are no “secret moves.” There is, however, a deep understanding of **how force truly works**.

### Goho (剛法) — the strength of directness

**Goho** are the direct, powerful techniques:
- strikes and kicks that transmit energy
- blocks that redirect incoming force
- counters that respond in an instant

Yet Goho is not brutality. It is **intelligent use of force**. You rely on dynamics and physics, not muscle alone. That is why a smaller person can control someone stronger.

**Outside the dōjō**: sometimes you must act directly, clearly, and with power. That is not wrong — it is necessary.

---

### Juho (柔法) — the strength of adaptation

**Juho** are the soft, flowing techniques:
- joint controls that manage the body
- throws that break balance
- evasions that redirect force

The opposite of force is not weakness — it is **adaptability**. Water is not hard like stone, yet over time it wears the stone away.

Juho teaches you to **move with the flow instead of fighting it**.

**In daily life**: not every problem yields to blunt force. Sometimes you need patience, understanding, and the ability to adapt to change.

---

### The balance: Ken Zen Ichinyo

The great insight of Shorinji Kempo is that **Goho and Juho are not separate — they complete each other**.

Picture this:
- **Teeth are hard (Goho)** — they can bite
- **Lips are soft (Juho)** — they can move
- **Together they hold the food**

You need both.

Over years of training, the two paths merge:
- you learn when force is appropriate
- you learn when adaptation serves better
- you develop **inner feel** — an intuitive sense of the moment

**This is more than technique. It is a way of reading the world.**

---

### Training for everyone

Shorinji Kempo welcomes all:

- **Children**: building confidence and physical fitness
- **Teens**: discovering inner strength and self-trust
- **Adults**: health, balance, inner calm
- **Beginners**: no prior experience required
- **Advanced students**: the techniques can deepen insight for a lifetime

Age, build, raw strength — none of that decides your path. **Shorinji Kempo works for you.**

---

## The reason behind it all

All these techniques, all this balance — **to what end?**

Not for competition. Not to dominate.

**To protect yourself. To protect others. To grow stronger — in body and in mind.**

This is Shorinji Kempo.
